Basera: premium home décor, from 35 SKUs to the 8 that sold

35 → 8SKUs
9Co-founders
S
A dropshipping venture in Masters' Union's venture challenge, launched with a 35-product catalogue and a team of 9.
T
Own strategy and operations, and how the brand looked and was perceived online.
A
Collected buyer feedback and asked people what they would buy, then ran weekly SKU management reviews. Benchmarked competitors to set premium pricing. Coordinated 3 vendors on fulfilment. Created daily posts and product visuals with Generative AI.
R
Cut the range to the 8 products customers actually chose, making ₹52.3K in month one and ~₹1.7L in three months.
Hardest moment

Co-founder conflicts stopped all work for 3 days. I brought everyone back together, and we came back with more energy.

What I'd do differently

Fewer co-founders, with clear ownership that doesn't overlap. Overlapping roles are where the conflict starts.

VANTAGE: compete with Blinkit on speed, or play a different game?

S
A business with 7 branches, merging into one. The choice: race Zepto and Blinkit on speed, or build a 3D mall experience.

Validate the idea and shape how the launch would look to customers.

Internship · Okaya EVJul – Aug 2024

"If they don't trust me, who would they trust?"

S
Okaya had a strong offline presence but was barely visible online. Even its sister brand Microtek was better known.
T
Get the dealer network listed on Google Business. Each listing needed OTPs from dealers who had never heard of me.
A
Email and WhatsApp got about 20% of dealers listed. Calls showed the real blocker was trust, not process. I traced the old dealership records and brought in the people who had originally signed each dealer.
R
350+ dealers onboarded, which is 99% of the network.
